consistent across the entire application. Translators and contributors must use
these exact forms — do **not** substitute alternatives or machine-translate them.

## Rules for Contributors

1. **Never translate the brand name** "FaithHaven" or "FaithHaven AI".
2. **Use this glossary** for all theological terms — consistency matters more
   than stylistic variation.
3. **Bible references** (e.g. "John 3:16") keep the book name in the target
   language but retain the chapter:verse numbering as-is.
4. **When in doubt**, consult the Bible Society of South Africa (BSSA) standard
   translations for isiZulu and isiXhosa terminology.
5. **AI-generated translations** must be reviewed by a native speaker before
   release, especially for isiZulu and isiXhosa.
